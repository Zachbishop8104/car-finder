# Code of Conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
The full text at that link applies here. In short:

## Our standards

Everyone taking part in this project is expected to help keep it open
and welcoming, whatever their background, identity or level of
experience.

Behaviour that helps:

- Being respectful of differing opinions and experience.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
- Owning mistakes and learning from them.
- Focusing on what is best for the people who use the project.

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional
  setting.

## Scope

This applies in the project's issues, pull requests, discussions and
any other space where someone is representing the project.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer privately, through the
contact options on their
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/Zachbishop8104), or by using
GitHub's "Report content" option on the comment or issue. Reports are
kept confidential.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and
other contributions that break this code, and may block people who
repeatedly or seriously do so.
